A little letter for you

My Love

There are some things that flowers can say better than I can. So I wanted to give you a little bouquet made of all the things I feel but don't always know how to say. The pink roses are for the tenderness you bring into my life. The tulips are for all the beautiful beginnings and possibilities that I hope we continue to discover together. The daisies are for the little moments — the laughs, the quiet conversations, the random memories — that somehow become my favorite ones. And the baby's breath is for the small things. Because sometimes love isn't only found in grand gestures. Sometimes it's in remembering. Sometimes it's in staying. Sometimes it's simply in choosing someone, again and again. And if you ever wonder what you mean to me... I hope this little bouquet reminds you. You are loved. You are appreciated. And you are someone I am very, very grateful to have met.
With all my heart, ♡
